Our Methodology

HOW BOREWELL DRILLING WORKS

From your first call to a fully functioning borewell — here is the exact process Sri Kannan Rig Service follows every time.

  1. 1

    Free Site Assessment

    Our experts visit your land, review local geological data, and assess surface conditions at zero cost. No commitment required.

  2. 2

    Soil Testing & Water Table Analysis

    Scientific analysis identifies water-bearing strata, rock formation type, and optimal drilling depth — critical for success in Tamil Nadu's hard-rock zones (Erode foothills, Palani hills).

  3. 3

    Transparent Drilling Quote

    You receive a detailed per-foot quote with estimated total cost, chosen casing size, and expected depth range — no hidden charges.

  4. 4

    Hydraulic Rig Drilling

    Modern hydraulic rigs drill to the target depth (100–500+ feet). Drilling takes 1–3 days depending on depth and soil type.

  5. 5

    Casing Installation & Well Development

    PVC or MS casing is fitted to prevent collapse. The well is developed by flushing to achieve stable, clean water yield before testing.

  6. 6

    Pump Installation & Handover

    Submersible pump is installed, pipes connected, and the complete borewell system tested. Full handover with maintenance guidance provided.

Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

What is the cost of borewell drilling in Tamil Nadu?
Borewell drilling in Tamil Nadu costs ₹40,000 to ₹2,00,000+ depending on depth, diameter, and soil type. A 4.75-inch domestic borewell at 100–200 feet typically costs ₹40,000–₹80,000. Deeper agricultural borewells at 300–500 feet range from ₹80,000–₹2,00,000. Sri Kannan Rig Service provides transparent per-foot pricing after a free site assessment — call +91 98427 25055 for an accurate quote.
How deep do borewells need to be in Erode and Tiruppur?
In Erode and Tiruppur, the water table typically lies between 150 to 500 feet depending on location, geology, and season. Hard-rock terrain near Erode's foothills often requires 300–500 ft. Perundurai and flat agricultural areas may find water at 150–250 ft. SKRS conducts soil testing to determine the exact optimal depth before drilling begins.
Do you offer borewell services near Palani and Chennaimalai?
Yes. SKRS operates in Palani, Chennaimalai, Perundurai and all surrounding areas. The team is fully mobile with rigs suited for the hard-rock geology near the Palani hills and Western Ghats foothills. We understand the specific drilling challenges of these regions from over 15 years of local experience.
How long does borewell drilling take?
A standard borewell drilling project takes 1 to 3 days: shallow borewells (100–200 ft, soft soil) are completed in 1 day; deep hard-rock borewells (300–500 ft) take 2–3 days. Soil testing and site preparation are done prior to drilling. SKRS works efficiently to minimise disruption to your property.
What borewell sizes does SKRS offer?
SKRS offers three standard borewell casing sizes: 4.75 inch (domestic/household use), 6.5 inch (small-scale agriculture and commercial), and 8 inch (large agriculture and industrial). The right size is recommended based on your water requirements, pump size, and anticipated yield. See our comparison table above for full details.
What is borewell flushing and when is it needed?
Borewell flushing is a high-pressure cleaning process using air and water to remove sediment, sand, and mineral scale from the borewell and screens. It is needed when water yield has dropped significantly, water appears muddy or has changed colour, or the submersible pump struggles to operate. SKRS recommends periodic flushing every 3–5 years for optimal borewell health.
Do you provide soil testing before borewell drilling?
Yes. Soil testing is the first step in every SKRS project. The analysis identifies water-bearing geological zones, rock type, water table depth, and optimal drilling approach for your specific land. This reduces drilling risk, prevents dry holes, and ensures value for money — especially important in Tamil Nadu's varied geology.
Is borewell water guaranteed?
No borewell contractor can guarantee water, as it depends on local geology and groundwater conditions. However, SKRS's 15+ years of experience and mandatory pre-drill soil testing significantly increase the probability of striking water at the right depth. Our team's deep knowledge of Tamil Nadu's regional geology is your best assurance of a successful outcome.
